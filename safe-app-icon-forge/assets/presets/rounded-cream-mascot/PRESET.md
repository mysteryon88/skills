# Rounded Cream Mascot preset

A friendly cream character with a very dark navy outline, dot eyes, a simple smile, and one replaceable app metaphor.

Keep the numeric contract in `style-lock.json`. Replace only the `app-metaphor` group in both SVG tiers, then update `visual-review.json` and provenance. The small artwork is an independent optical redraw: larger eyes and gaps, thicker contours, fewer details, and a larger foreground metaphor.

The universal workflow does not require this preset.
